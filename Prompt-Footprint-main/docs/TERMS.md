## 1. What PromptFootprint is

PromptFootprint is a free browser extension that estimates the environmental
impact of your AI chat usage, checks your writing, and suggests shorter prompts.
By installing or using it, you agree to these terms.

## 2. The estimates are estimates

The energy, water, and CO₂ figures are **approximations** derived from public
disclosures and published research, not direct measurements of your specific
requests. They are meant to build intuition and encourage efficient prompting.
Do not treat them as audited or authoritative. See "How it works" in the dashboard
and [../METHODOLOGY.md](../METHODOLOGY.md) for the method and its limitations.
